How it works

From sticker to insight in about five minutes.

  1. Create a tag for each spot. A table, a counter, a treatment room — whatever your customers sit at or stand in front of.
  2. Write the link onto an NFC sticker. Do it from your own Android phone in the dashboard, or print the QR fallback while the stickers are in the post.
  3. The customer taps and rates. No app, no download, no account. The page opens by itself.
  4. You read it the same evening. Every rating, comment and contact detail, grouped by spot, category and day.
