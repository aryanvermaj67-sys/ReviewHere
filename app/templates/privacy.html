How your data is used

Last updated when this page was deployed.

What is collected when you leave a review

  • Your star rating, any category ratings, and your written comment.
  • Your name and contact details — only if you type them in. Both are optional.
  • Which tag you tapped — which table, counter or desk — and the time of the tap.
  • Your browser's user-agent string, and your IP address for a short window, used to stop the form being spammed.

No cookies are set on the guest review page, and there are no third-party trackers or analytics scripts on it.

Who can see it

Only the business you reviewed. Reviews are private to that business's dashboard — they are not published on this site, not shown to other businesses, and not sold or shared with anyone. Guest review pages are marked noindex, so search engines do not list them.

Posting publicly is your choice

If you rate a business highly, you may be offered a button to post your review on Google. That is optional, it opens Google separately, and nothing you wrote here is sent there automatically — you write the public review yourself.

Removing your review

Ask the business you reviewed, or email {% if support %}{{ support }}{% else %}the operator of this site{% endif %} with roughly when you visited and which business. Reviews are deleted on request, along with any contact details attached to them.
